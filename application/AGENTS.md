# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Design decisions from the user (Aavas pricing POC)

- Area manager review: the Decision tab shows everything in full (upsell summary, pricing rationale, your decision) with no separate "Decision support" card; the other tabs keep detail folded behind one-line summaries and "Show all".
- Frame rate requests from the upsell side: holding the model rate is the upsell over the RM's ask (rate, acceptance, difference); cost of a discount is the interest over the full loan, never per year.
- No "beyond your limit" anywhere; no Deep dive link on the approval list (it lives inside the review).
- Plain business language, no abbreviations ("System recommendation" for the recommended rate - never "Model reco", "model rate" or "Model recommendation"): rates in per cent and acceptance in per cent (changes in percentage points), not "N in 100", basis points or model jargon.
- Relationship managers see the price and band, not the analysis behind it; no audit trail, policy page or EMI calculator in the menus.
- The suggested decision (what to do, 3-4 facts, one click fills the form) lives on the Decision support tab only; the Decision tab no longer opens with it (removed at the user's request, 2026-10-28). Decline leads when the suggestion is to hold the model rate.
- The RM's In progress list shows real files only: the sample leads in config/intake.yaml `sample_in_progress` were removed at the user's request (2026-10-28).
- Pricing intelligence (was "Price & scenarios") is one review tab (price and acceptance combined with scenario analysis), opening with "How the prediction is made, and how this branch differs", then the price and acceptance curve, comparing every branch (the model) with this branch; acceptance figures come from scoring the exact rates, so every screen shows the same numbers. It then has a "Best offer" section (BestOffer.jsx, `/best-offer`, settings in config/best_offer.yaml; decision support, the AM's decision still changes only the rate): the RM's ask, the recommendation and the best offers by objective (most value, most likely to close, cheapest way to match the ask) over four levers - rate, loan amount, tenure, processing fee - in one comparison table with a plain sentence each, a chart of every eligible offer with the frontier, what each lever buys on its own, and what fell outside policy and outside the EMI comfort limit (a limit set in best_offer.yaml, not a policy rule; offers with a changed tenure or a smaller loan are flagged because the acceptance model has not seen them). Then a "Risk-aware pricing" section (RiskPricing.jsx, `/risk-pricing`; decision support only, never sets the price): a verdict, risk score tiles, where the PD sits in its grade band with the neighbouring grades' card rates, the top factors moving the PD, every rate with EMI, FOIR, stressed FOIR against the grade cap, PD rescored at that rate, margin over risk cost, acceptance and value, and the grade against the book (rates, defaults, percentile of the ask and recommendation). It replaced the one-line grade sentence in the deep dive. Its rate table runs on `analytics/offer_evaluator.py`, the shared four-lever (rate, amount, tenure, fee) evaluator.
- Area manager review has a Decision support tab after Decision: an analysis table, not cards (the user preferred it) - each factor with its figure, what it means and a signal (for approving / no objection / caution / against / for information), grouped as need for the discount, cost to the bank, and branch context and precedent. Made for reading and quick use: a one-line verdict ('The case for the discount is weak'), four key figures (the ask, acceptance change, interest given up, margin left), the suggested decision with 'Use this decision' (docked at the bottom once the summary scrolls away), 'For approving / Against approving / Watch' lists whose factor names jump to their row, a filter (All / Needs attention / Against approving / No concern) and section links, rows with a coloured edge and an icon per signal, 'See in <tab>' links that say where they lead, and the calculator and the alternatives opened on demand.
- Risk and model validation has an "RBI Artefacts" page: each RBI direction, circular or draft that bears on home-loan pricing and model governance, its asks, what the platform does and live evidence, with coverage (in place / partly / gap / outside) and gaps or failing evidence listed first. Drafts and committee reports are labelled as such, never as law in force.
- Relationship manager files are filled in three steps named in plain words, each unlocking the next: "Indicative pricing" (the customer's own figures, sense-checked, e.g. loan to value within policy), "Compute loan risk" (every verified figure and both checks; no risk-adjusted rate before), and "Customer interaction and negotiation" (negotiation, relationship, conversation notes; a competitor quote is optional; no acceptance rate before). Never "customer behaviour". Nothing is sent to the customer or the area manager until all three are complete; the server refuses it too.
- The RM's offer form has no note field and no competitor fact check. Any rate below the RM's authority can be sent to the area manager, even below the lowest rate policy allows; the RM is told where the policy floor is, and the area manager is told what can still be approved (down to their limit and the floor) or that only a decline is possible.
- Area manager sees the loan and its EMI on the Decision tab: loan amount, tenure, property, loan to value, income and other EMIs, then the EMI, total interest and all EMIs as a share of income at the RM's ask and the system recommendation. (This replaced the "How the price was reached" cards at the user's request.) The approvals list shows the indicative range beside the rate to hold.
- The RM sees the final acceptance rate: how many in 100 similar customers accept at the rate in the offer box (live, beside the recommendation's), and at a rate already sent or asked of the area manager.
- Application status for the RM and the area manager: not a menu of its own but an "All applications · status" view inside My Applications (RM) and Approval requests (area manager) (every file, its status, indicative range, risk-adjusted rate, acceptance, the next possible action and whose move it is - RM, area manager or customer - with an Open file or Review button, and its steps on demand), and the same step-by-step status on the RM's file and at the top of the manager's review.
- The final price stays realistic against the indicative range: the optimizer searches about ±0.30% around the Stage-2 market-practice rate instead of walking every file to the top of its quoted range; it leaves the range only where policy forces it, and then only to the nearest permissible rate. No upsell room is shown once policy has priced above the quote. Upsell room is the highest rate at which 70% of similar customers still accept, capped at the top of the quoted range and the card rate; where no rate reaches 70%, there is no room, and the quoted top never stands in for one.
- Discount room is wide enough to demo the RM-to-manager flow: the lowest rate is the card rate less 0.60% (`absolute_discount_floor_bps`, HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14), never below 13.00%; the RM may go 0.25% below the recommendation with a reason, the area manager approves down to 1.00% below it. A request is decided under the policy in force, not the one it was priced under.
- Price uplift for the area manager: besides approving or declining a lower rate, the manager can decline the discount and price above the recommendation, up to the rate customers like this still accept and never above the range quoted at Stage 1 or the card rate (HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14 allows no premium above the matrix rate), with an uplift reason and a note; the offer goes to the customer at that rate and it is recorded as a premium override. The suggested decision proposes an uplift when acceptance falls no more than 3 points and the loan is worth at least 0.5% more.
- Worklists carry each file's status with the next action and its owner, aging (days at the current step, days open) and the upsell view (holding the rate over the ask in bps, room above the rate to hold, or the top of the quoted range before pricing): the RM's In progress, Ready, Pending and Sent tables and the area manager's Approval requests list.
- One pricing policy only: the policy in force (HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14) with no archived versions shown; demo files are priced under it.
- Decision support reads every factor against the RM's ask, in simple words: "Positive for the RM's ask", "Negative for the RM's ask", "Be watchful", "Verify before approving"; signals Positive / OK / Verify / Watchful / Negative / Info; "What it means" in short plain sentences that say what the number means and what to do.
