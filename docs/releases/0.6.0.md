# Release 0.6.0 (2026-11-19)

Previous release: 0.5.0 (HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14). Pricing behaviour is unchanged in this
release: the same policy, grade matrix and models price every file.

## Highlights

- **Agentic OS.** The models are rebuilt as a workflow: data, policy checks, training,
  serving. Training runs in-process, on an Amazon SageMaker training job, or, for
  challengers only, with the AutoGluon Assistant agent. Promoting a result to champion
  stays a separate, approved step.
- **Serving on SageMaker Serverless.** Endpoint `aavas-pricing-bundle` (ap-south-1,
  3 GB) serves all 8 models. Before the step passes, the endpoint is called with
  training's reference rows and must answer exactly as training did; it does for all
  8 (first call 704 ms).
- **Drift monitoring.** Data, model and concept drift are detected against the training
  window and shipped to OpenObserve. There, three alerts and a dashboard watch them, and
  alerts post back into the platform. A simulated price war fires all three alerts.
- **RBI controls.**
  - A Key Facts Statement with the APR for every offer, and written reasons for every
    rejection (Fair Practices Code).
  - A model inventory with risk tiers and independent validation records (draft MRM
    guidance 2026, FREE-AI).
  - An RBI register of 52 paragraph-cited requirements with live evidence.

## Upgrade steps

1. `pip install -r requirements.txt` (adds `boto3`, `botocore[crt]`, `httpx`).
2. Restart the API. The two new tables (`kfs_statements`, `model_validations`) are
   created on start; no data migration.
3. Drift monitoring with OpenObserve (optional):
   1. Create `.env.openobserve` from the keys in `config/monitoring.yaml`. It is
      git-ignored; never commit it.
   2. Start OpenObserve.
   3. Run `python scripts/run_drift.py --provision` once.
4. SageMaker (optional):
   1. Set `AAVAS_SAGEMAKER_ROLE_ARN` and `AAVAS_SAGEMAKER_BUCKET` in the environment or
      in the git-ignored `.env`; `.env.example` shows the shape. They are not in
      `config/agentic_os.yaml` because they carry the AWS account number and the
      repository is public.
   2. Sign in with `aws login`.
5. Decisions priced before this release carry `app_version` 0.1.0; from now on they
   carry the real version.

## Known issues and open decisions

- **LTV ceiling (compliance gap).** The policy allows 90% LTV on loans of ₹30-75 lakh,
  where RBI's limit is 80% (HFC Directions para 58), and two booked loans exceed it.
  Fixing it needs a policy version with tiered LTV caps. Decision pending.
- **KFS charges.** Penal charges, the benchmark and its reset period are placeholders
  until the Board and the ALCO approve them; every KFS says "illustrative" until then.
  The KFS is in English; a Hindi KFS is marked for translation.
- **Model risk.**
  - No model has been independently validated yet.
  - The inventory has not been reviewed.
  - The pricing models have no per-model kill switch.
  - Conversion, prepayment and uplift fail their fairness tests.
  - Nothing has been red-teamed.
- **Governance monitors** (drift, retraining, override review, fairness) have never been
  run: nothing schedules them.
- **AutoGluon Assistant.** It is blocked until the Anthropic use-case form is submitted
  in the Bedrock console. It also runs model-written code unsandboxed, so it needs a
  container with Bedrock-only credentials before use beyond a developer machine.
- **Stage-1 quote band.**
  - It covers the final price 56.8% of the time, short of the level promised; the
    band-width cap is a pending business decision.
  - For some files the quote sits below the file's own policy floor. In one test the
    quote was 13.85–15.10% and the final rate 15.40%. The policy wins, so the final rate
    lands above what the applicant was quoted.
  - Two indicative-band tests fail on this and are left failing until the Stage-1
    decision is made.
- **Optional packages** `shap`, `omnixai`, `xgboost`, `lightgbm` and `anthropic` are not
  installed in the local environment, so 28 tests that need them fail here.
- **No screens yet** for the KFS and model risk; the RBI register screen shows the new rows.
- **EC2 hosting** is waiting on the server key.
