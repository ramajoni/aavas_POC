# Release 0.9.3 (2026-11-21)

Previous release: 0.9.2. A patch release of the web application. The API, the policy
(HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14) and the models are unchanged.

## Highlights

- **The "Further upsell room" tile is gone from the area manager's request summary.**
  Under 2026.14 it read "No room - Fewer than 70% of customers like this accept even at
  the system recommendation" on most files, which gave the manager nothing to act on. The
  summary keeps three tiles: the ask against the recommendation, the upsell by holding the
  recommendation, and the extra interest over the loan.
- **Still shown elsewhere.** The price-uplift line in the decision panel (only where
  customers like this accept a higher rate), the "Upsell room" column of the approvals table
  and the "Upsell room" tile on a file's history page are unchanged.

## Changes

### Removed
- The "Further upsell room" tile on the area manager's request summary (Approvals). It
  showed "No room" with "Fewer than 70% of customers like this accept even at the system
  recommendation" on most files under HL-LAP-POLICY-2026.14, which told the manager nothing
  they could act on. The summary now has three tiles: ask against recommendation, the upsell
  by holding the recommendation, and the extra interest over the loan. The price-uplift line
  in the decision panel still appears where customers like this accept a higher rate.

## Upgrade steps

Web only: build the application (`npm run build` in `application/`) and publish
`dist/client` to the web root. The API needs no restart.

## Known issues

- Everything listed under Known issues in 0.9.2 still stands.
